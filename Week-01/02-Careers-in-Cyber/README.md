# Careers in Cyber

## TryHackMe Room

**Room:** Careers in Cyber

**Platform:** TryHackMe

## Objective

The objective of this room was to explore different career paths and roles within the cybersecurity field and understand the skills and responsibilities associated with them.

## What I Learned

In this room, I explored different areas of cybersecurity and learned that the field offers a wide range of career opportunities.

I learned about different cybersecurity roles and the types of skills and responsibilities associated with security professionals.

The room also helped me understand that cybersecurity is not limited to penetration testing. It includes multiple domains such as defensive security, offensive security, security analysis, incident response, digital forensics, governance and other specialized areas.

## Key Concepts

- Cybersecurity career paths
- Offensive security
- Defensive security
- Security analysis
- Incident response
- Digital forensics
- Cybersecurity skills and roles

## Key Takeaways

- Cybersecurity is a broad field with many career opportunities.
- Different cybersecurity roles require different technical and analytical skills.
- Hands-on practice is important for developing cybersecurity skills.
- Understanding different career paths can help in choosing an area of specialization.
